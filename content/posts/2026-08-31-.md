[Kill The Cookie Banner](https://killthecookiebanner.eu/) is a consortium trying to influence the EU to update its data processing rules to make privacy controls a browser-level preference instead of a per-site preference. (via [Nick Heer](https://pxlnv.com/linklog/kill-the-cookie-banner/))
